**File**: [GuiaInformes.pdf](../raw/Guias%20de%20Formato/GuiaInformes.pdf)  
**Date Ingested**: 2026-04-13

## Summary
This guide fixes the expectations for the written report. It stresses that the report must be self-contained, technically written, logically structured, and fully supported by cited figures, equations, and references.

## Key Takeaways
- The report and presentation are independent documents.
- Sections and subsections must be numbered.
- Use technical language and avoid informal prose.
- Every section must have explanatory text; no loose figures.
- Figures and equations must be numbered and referenced from the text.
- References belong in a final section and only include cited works.
