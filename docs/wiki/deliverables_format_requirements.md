# Analysis: Deliverable Formatting Requirements

These rules matter because the implementation and the final presentation are graded separately. The source guides should be treated as operational constraints, not as optional style suggestions.

## Report Rules
- The report must be self-contained and independent from the presentation.
- Sections and subsections must be numbered.
- Use technical language and one consistent language throughout the document.
- Figures and equations must be numbered and referenced from the text.
- The report needs a final `Referencias` section containing only works cited in the text.

## Presentation Rules
- The statement of the TP has maximum priority over generic guides.
- Slides must stay concise; do not paste long paragraphs or bibliography text.
- Use a coherent section structure: introduction, implementation, simulations, results, conclusions.
- Animations in the submitted PDF must appear as a representative still frame plus an explicit external link.
- Conclusions must be based only on results actually shown.

## Statistical Reporting Rules
- When reporting averages, pair them with their associated uncertainty.
- Respect significant figures implied by the error magnitude.
- Prefer explicit units and readable axis labels.
