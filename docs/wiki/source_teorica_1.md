## Summary
This lecture situates molecular dynamics as the natural numerical approach for many-particle systems when analytical solutions stop being practical. Its main value for the repo is conceptual: `Sistema 1` is not an isolated kinematics exercise but a many-body simulation problem.

## Key Takeaways
- One- and two-body systems are usually analytically tractable; many-body systems are not.
- Molecular dynamics is the relevant computational lens for large interacting particle sets.
- The lecture also frames presentation expectations, but those are captured more directly in the dedicated format guides.

## Impact on This Repo
- It reinforces the modeling choice behind [System 1: Scanning Rate](system_1_scanning_rate.md).
- It helps justify the clean separation between physical model, numerical implementation, and result reporting.
