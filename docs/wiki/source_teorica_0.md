## Summary
This lecture is the main statistical reporting reference for the TP. It emphasizes repeated realizations, sample-error reporting, significant figures consistent with uncertainty, and the interpretation of averaged simulation outputs.

## Key Takeaways
- Stochastic simulations should be repeated with different seeds or realizations.
- Report observables as mean plus uncertainty, typically standard deviation when appropriate.
- Significant figures must be consistent with the uncertainty magnitude.
- Histograms and regression models are presentation tools, not substitutes for defining the observable correctly.
